## NEW

- MEGA RAIDS: up to 8 players against one giant boss, in stages. Clear a stage and everyone picks an evolved weapon.
- Five raid bosses on rotation, three days each: THE ECLIPSE, THE LEVIATHAN, THE HIVE, THE STORMHEART and THE CLOCKWORK.
- Raid tickets: earn them by clearing raid stages and spend them on each boss’s own banner of limited skins.
- Lobby chat with QUICK CHAT buttons, plus MUTE and REPORT. Choose ALL, QUICK ONLY or OFF in SETTINGS.
- A much longer tutorial: your first run and your first visit to each screen explain what everything does.
- This update screen: you will see every change, and a big button when a new version is ready.

## CHANGES

- RARE ROLL: it can now give anything the standard roll gives, plus a 10% EPIC and a 5% CHROMA chance. Every 10th roll is a guaranteed epic and every 30th a guaranteed chroma, whatever you get in between.
- Tickets bought with VOIDBUX have no daily limit: standard, rare, event and raid tickets. Only shard tickets keep their daily limit.
- The STANDARD ROLL’s odds are unchanged, but its shards and voidbux now burst out of the ticket and fly into your counter.
- Menus tidied for phones: bigger buttons, nothing overlapping on short screens, and the Android back button always backs out of a menu.

## ALSO IN THIS SEASON

- SHIELDED ENEMIES: elites and Wardkeepers carry hex wards that soak damage. Some weapons break wards faster.
- PIERCING BOLTS: some enemy shots go straight through shields and bullet-clearing ultimates. They glow and spark.
- THE HORDE HARDENS: the stronger your build, the tougher new enemies arrive. Your weapons are never nerfed.
- Pet levels, RUN BOOSTS, KILL EFFECTS and SHOT COLOURS in the shop, and the ELITE skins.

## FIXES

- Walls that move during a boss now reach every player in co-op (this also fixes THE INTERRED in THE UNDERVAULT).
- The name and lobby-code boxes now work with a phone keyboard.
