# THE ROT — galaxy design

> *Everything that grows goes here in the end.*

The IMPOSSIBLE galaxy, and the last one. Twelve worlds and a core, built the same
way as the other three: every world is one ordinary 10:30 run with its own locals,
its own sky, its own finale boss and a relic to win. Everything in it is new — no
enemy, elite or boss from another galaxy appears here.

Theme: what is under the garden. Graves, compost, mould, mushrooms, flies, bone,
the threads a fungus runs through dead wood — decay, and the strange, patient life
that feeds on it. Sickly light: bone white, bile green, bruise violet, spore pink,
rust.

THE SHALLOWS teaches one idea per boss. THE THICKET combines them. THE ROT is the
**exam**: each finale is built around an idea that punishes one specific bad habit
— running away, standing still, fighting from far off, killing in the wrong place,
ignoring the small things — and asks for it at IMPOSSIBLE tempo. Impossible
already shortens every warning, so every attack here has a shape you learn once
and can read at a glance: sectors are scythes, rectangles are graves, circles on
the ground are footfalls, threads are threads.

Impossible already multiplies every boss's bullets and shortens every pause, so the
Rot's bosses throw a little less than the garden's do at the same difficulty: they
attack about a fifth less often and their spirals are shorter. The pressure comes from
what each fight asks of you, not from the number of bullets on screen.

## How it fits

| Galaxy | Difficulty | Opens when |
|---|---|---|
| THE SHALLOWS | Easy | always open |
| THE GARDEN | Medium | always open |
| THE THICKET | Hard | you put out THE SOWER at EVENTIDE |
| **THE ROT** | **Impossible** | **you bring down THE HUNTER at THE HEARTWOOD** |

- Relics from any galaxy can be equipped anywhere. The two-relic limit stays.
- The Rot's gauntlet draws from its own three wild bosses, plus the finales of
  worlds you have taken in THE ROT.

## The rank and file (12)

Each fills the role one of THE GARDEN's enemies fills, so the spawn director, the
surges and the elite rotation all still hold. What each one *does* is new.

| Enemy | Role (garden slot) | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **SHAMBLER** | baseline chaser (Drifter) | A dead thing on its feet. Lurches: a burst of speed, then a stagger. |

| **BLOAT** | splitter (Splitter) | A swollen corpse. Bursts into a cloud of Gnats. |

## The finales — one bad habit each

**THE SEXTON** · Potter's Field · ★1 — *GRAVES.* A gravedigger with a spade. It
measures you — a grave-shaped outline under your feet — and digs. Open graves stay
for the rest of the fight: step in one and you are slowed and hurt. It throws
clods of earth that land where they are marked. Later it digs two at once, then
dives into a grave and climbs out of another. **Habit: fighting wherever you
happen to be. Keep your ground clear; lead it away from the graves.**

**THE HARVESTMAN** · Stubblefield · ★1 — *GAIT.* A small body high on eight very
long legs. It walks by planting feet, one at a time, in order — each footfall
marked on the ground a moment before it lands. The body is the only thing you can
hurt, and it only drops low enough to be hit while it stands. **Habit: watching the
body. Watch where the next foot goes.** Later it runs, and its footfalls throw
rings.

**THE RAVENER** · The Midden · ★2 — *APPETITE.* Enormous, and hungry. A ring round
it shows its reach: shots from outside the ring are swallowed and do a fifth of
their damage. It spews rot in cones, and it eats every corpse that falls inside
its ring to heal. **Habit: fighting from far off. Get inside, and don't feed it.**

**THE FAIRY RING** · Toadstool Hollow · ★2 — *THE RING.* A pale crowned mushroom.
It sprouts a ring of caps around you (the ring shows first, then the caps rise),
and the ring tightens. You can't walk out through it — but any one cap can be
broken, and one broken cap opens the ring. **Habit: waiting it out. Break out.**
Later the rings come nested, two deep.

**THE MYCELIUM** · Underloam · ★2 — *THE NETWORK.* Not one body: a spread of
fruiting caps, joined by threads that hurt to touch. Only one cap at a time is
alive — it glows — and the heart moves through the threads to another every few
seconds. Hurting a dark cap does nothing. **Habit: shooting what is nearest. Go to
the one that matters.**

**THE UNBURIED** · The Lychgate · ★3 — *IT RISES.* Every time you bring it down it
falls, lies still, and pulls the dead of the arena into itself — and gets up
stronger for every corpse it took. Stand on it while it lies there and it
can't take any. **Habit: celebrating early. Finish it.**

**THE GIBBET** · Gallows Hill · ★3 — *THE SWING.* A cage on a chain, swinging from
somewhere far above. It swings across the arena as a pendulum — you can hear the
creak at the top of each swing — and the thing inside shoots out through the bars.
**Habit: moving on instinct. Move on the beat.** Later the chain lengthens and
the arc widens; then it swings in a circle.

**THE SPOREMOTHER** · Mildew · ★3 — *CLEAN AIR.* It breathes spore fog in from the
edges of the arena. Only a few pockets of clean air are left open, marked by
light, and the fog moves. Standing in fog hurts. **Habit: holding your ground. Go
where the air is.**

**THE EFFIGY** · Cenotaph · ★4 — *DON'T RUN.* A stone mourner off a tomb. It moves only
while you move *away* from it — and then it moves fast. Walk toward it, or
across it, and it is stone. Its tears fall as rain on marked ground. Its
touch is the hardest hit in the Rot. **Habit: running. Face it.**

**THE PLAGUE** · The Withering · ★5 — *GIVE IT BACK.* It marks you with a bubo: a
ring round you that counts down. When it runs out it bursts — a wide blast that
hurts every enemy in it, and leaves a cloud of sickness where you stood. Carry it
to THE PLAGUE and let it burst there, then get out of the cloud. **Habit:
keeping away from the thing you are fighting.** *(holds THE ASH)*

**THE PALLBEARERS** · Mourning Road · ★5 — *DIVIDE.* Four hooded bearers, carrying a
coffin, in step. Each has its own life, and each fights its own way: one throws,
one swings, one calls the dead, one walls the road. Put one down and the others
carry faster. When the last falls, the lid lifts, and THE BURIED climbs out
for the final stretch. **Habit: spreading your fire. Take them one at a time.**
*(holds THE NAIL)*

**THE CORDYCEPS** · Puppetwood · ★5 — *STRINGS.* It gets into the others. Any enemy it
touches with a spore becomes its puppet: tinted, strung to it, and damage to a
puppet is damage to it — but while it has puppets it can't be hurt directly.
When it pulls the strings, every puppet fires at once. **Habit: ignoring the small
things. Their numbers are its weakness.** *(holds THE THREAD)*

## The core — THE BARROW

*Where nothing grows until something dies.*

Sealed until you hold all three pieces of **the Grave Key** — THE ASH, THE NAIL and
THE THREAD, from the three hardest worlds. Like EVENTIDE, there are no trash
waves: only the thing that waits at the end of every garden, in four forms, each
held a while before it breaks.

THE SOWER planted the garden. This is the other half.

**THE REAPER**
- **I · THE REAPER** — a robe and a scythe. The scythe sweeps a wide sector (the
  sector shows first); the only safe ground in the swing is right up close to it,
  inside the blade. It **reaps** the motes you left lying and throws them back at you.
- **II · THE BLIGHT** — the robe falls empty and the rot comes out of it: arms of
  blight grow out from the middle and turn slowly, and spore bursts go off in
  the gaps between them.
- **III · THE BONEYARD** — it gathers every bone in the ground into one body. Graves
  open in rings round you, and the dead climb out of them.
- **IV · THE SEED** — all that is left: one seed. Tiny, and fast, and everything
  that grows starts from it. Where its shots land, thorns sprout for a few seconds.

When THE SEED breaks, something green comes up through THE BARROW.

## Relics

The Rot's relics are the strongest in the game, and the most expensive.

| Relic | Upside | Downside |
|---|---|---|
| **SPADEWORK** | Every kill within 200px takes 10% off your dash cooldown. | Dash cooldown +30%. |
| **LONGSTRIDE** | Move 12% faster. | Enemy bullets move 8% faster. |
| **OVERRIPE** | Motes are worth 50% more. | Motes rot away 6s after they fall. |
| **FAIRY RING** | Every 12s a ring of caps springs up round you for 3s. Nothing can walk into it. | Pickup radius −20%. |
| **MYCORRHIZA** | A quarter of every hit spreads to each other enemy within 120px of the target. | Damage −12%. |
| **DEATH RATTLE** | Deal up to +40% damage as your health falls. | Healing −30%. |
| **SWING** | Fire rate swings between +30% and −15% on a four-second beat. | *(the swing is the cost)* |
| **BLOATING** | One kill in five bursts, hurting everything within 110px. | You take +10% damage. |
| **EFFIGY** | Standing still turns you to stone: 35% less damage taken. | Move 10% slower. |
| **CANKER** | Enemies you hit rot for 3s, losing 2% of their health a second (bosses 0.25%). | Damage −10%. |
| **PALLBEARER** | A big hit (over 15% of your max HP) arrives in four parts over 2s, and the last part never lands. | Healing −20%. |
| **CORDYCEPS** | When you kill something, a spore jumps to the nearest enemy within 200px and deals half the dead one's max health. | Damage −10%. |

## Prizes

- **BARROW** — mastery skin, for putting down THE REAPER: dark loam, and one green
  shoot coming up through it.
- **PUFFCAP** — a pet: a very small mushroom that puffs a little cloud of spores
  when you dash. It thinks you are a tree.
- **Achievements** — a THE ROT group: first world, every world, the Grave Key, the
  core, and one for each finale's habit broken cleanly.
