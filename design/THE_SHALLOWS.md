# THE SHALLOWS — galaxy design

> *Where the garden meets the water.*

The EASY galaxy. Twelve worlds and a core, built the same way as THE GARDEN and
THE THICKET: every world is one ordinary 10:30 run with its own locals, its own
sky, its own finale boss and a relic to win. Everything in it is new — no enemy,
elite or boss from another galaxy appears here.

Theme: the warm, bright edge of the sea. Tide pools, kelp, coral, shells, jellies,
rays and whales — sunlight through water, and things that glow in it.

It is the galaxy a new player starts in, so it is built to **teach**. Every finale
is built around **one** idea, shown clearly, with long warnings. By the time you
reach its core you have learned, one world at a time, every skill the harder
galaxies will ask of you: damage windows, standing out of lingering hazards,
reading lines, focusing a target, dashing through an attack, swimming against a
pull, reading rotating hazards, and threading rings.

Nothing here reads your aim — your weapons fire themselves. Every lesson is about
**where you stand and when you dash**.

| Galaxy | Difficulty | Opens when |

Each fills the role one of THE GARDEN's enemies fills, so the spawn director, the

## The finales — one idea each

**THE HERMIT** · Sandbar · ★1 — *SHELL.* It spends half its time out of its shell,
slow and throwing pebbles, and half tucked in: nearly immune and scuttling to a new
spot. The shell glows before it hides. **Lesson: hit it in the window.**
Later it sprays from inside the shell too, and its windows get shorter.

**THE BELL** · Glasswater · ★1 — *PULSE.* A great jellyfish that moves in lunges. Every
pulse leaves a ring of stings hanging in the water for a few seconds.
**Lesson: don't stand where it has been.** Later it buds two small bells.

**THE EEL** · Kelpwood · ★2 — *CURRENT.* It plants kelp posts round the arena; lines of
current hum between them (a thin warning) and then fire. **Lesson: read the lines
and step between them — or dash through.** Later the posts move with it.

**THE URCHIN** · Urchin Flats · ★2 — *SPINES.* It grows spines, shown on a meter. At
full it bursts in a dense ring. Every hit knocks spines off. **Lesson: focus it
down — stay close enough that it's what your weapons are shooting.** Its bristling
little ones are there to steal your aim.

**THE STARFISH** · Starfall · ★2 — *ARMS.* Each fifth of its life, an arm tears off and
crawls back to regrow. **Dash across a crawling arm to crush it.** An arm that
makes it home heals the Starfish. **Lesson: the dash is a weapon.**

**THE MAELSTROM** · The Whirl · ★3 — *PULL.* It draws everything toward its middle —
you, and its own bullets, which curl inward. **Lesson: keep swimming against it.**

**THE MANTIS** · Smashing Reef · ★3 — *PUNCH.* A mantis shrimp. Its club glows, a lane
lights up, and the punch is the fastest thing in the Shallows — and it cracks the
water where it lands. **Lesson: dash through it at the last moment.** Later it
punches twice, then three times.

**THE OCTOPUS** · Inkwell · ★3 — *SWEEP.* It lays arms out across the arena (a warning
first) and sweeps them round like the hands of a clock. **Lesson: keep ahead of a
rotating hazard.** Later more arms, and ink that slows.

**THE RAY** · The Reaches · ★4 — *GLIDE.* It leaves, and comes back as a pass across
the arena: first its shadow (the warning lane), then the ray, trailing a wake of
bubbles. **Lesson: read where something is going, not where it is.**

**THE NAUTILUS** · Nautilus Shelf · ★5 — *CHAMBERS.* Spirals of shot from its
siphon. Each phase it retreats a chamber deeper: smaller, faster, one more spiral
arm. **Lesson: move *with* a spiral, not across it.** *(holds THE SPIRAL)*

**THE CLAM** · Pearl Beds · ★5 — *PEARL.* It opens and throws its pearl, which
ricochets round the arena and comes home along a line you can see. It can only be
hurt while it waits, open. **Lesson: watch the return path.** Later two pearls.
*(holds THE NACRE)*

**THE WHALE** · Whalefall · ★5 — *SONG.* Enormous, circling at the edge. Its song goes
out in slow rings with a gap in each; then it dives and **breaches** under you (a
wide warning circle). **Lesson: everything you learned, at once, gently.**
*(holds THE SONG)*

## Wild bosses (the Shallows' gauntlet)

| Boss | What it does |
|---|---|
| **THE GULL** | Circles high, then *dives* along a lane (its shadow warns you). It steals motes you leave lying around — kill it and it drops them all. |
| **THE KING CRAB** | Only ever moves sideways. Its shell is on its front (hits from ahead lose most of their damage) — get round it. Its claws snap in crossing lanes. |
| **THE SCHOOL** | A thousand fish on one life. It changes shape: a **ring** that closes on you (find the gap), an **arrow** that runs you through (a lane), a **wall** that sweeps across. |

## The core — MOONPOOL

*A moon, sunk to the bottom of a pool. The tide still answers it.*

Sealed until you hold all three pieces of **the Tide Key** — THE SPIRAL, THE NACRE and
THE SONG, from the three hardest worlds. Like EVENTIDE, there are no trash waves:
only the thing at the bottom, in four forms, each held a while before it breaks.

**THE MOTHER OF PEARL**
- **I · THE SHELL** — a clam the size of the pool. Open: it is soft, and it throws
  pearls. Shut: it is stone, and the tide goes out from it in rings.
- **II · THE TIDE** — no shell left, only water. Waves sweep across the arena as walls
  with one or two gaps in them.
- **III · THE PEARL** — all lustre. Beams of light turn round it like a lighthouse,
  and its shots skip once off the wall.
- **IV · THE GRAIN** — the grain of sand the pearl grew round. Tiny, and quick.

## Relics

| Relic | Upside | Downside |
|---|---|---|
| **SPARE SHELL** | Once a minute, a hit that would take a quarter of your health is blocked. | Move speed −8%. |
| **STINGER** | Enemies that touch you are stung (burn) for 3s. | Max HP −10%. |
| **STATIC** | Every 4th hit arcs to a second enemy nearby for half damage. | Fire rate −10%. |
| **SPINEBACK** | Being hit throws eight spines out of you. | You take +10% damage. |
| **REGROWTH** | Regain 2 HP a second while no enemy is within 250px. | Max HP −10%. |
| **UNDERTOW** | Pickup radius +100%. | Enemies move 8% faster. |
| **CLUBSTRIKE** | Your dash strikes what it passes through for heavy damage. | Dash cooldown +20%. |
| **INKSAC** | Being hit clears enemy bullets within 180px. | Max HP −10%. |
| **GLIDE** | Take 15% less damage while moving. | Take 15% more while standing still. |
| **BUOYANCY** | Levels come 12% faster. | Enemies have +10% health. |
| **PEARL** | A pearl circles you and eats one enemy bullet every 2s. | Pickup radius −20%. |
| **SONAR** | Every 10s, a pulse pushes back everything within 260px. | Dash cooldown +25%. |

## Prizes

- **NACRE** — mastery skin, for bringing down THE MOTHER OF PEARL.
- **SEED PEARL** — a pet: a tiny pearl in half a shell that catches the light.
- **Achievements** — a THE SHALLOWS group: first world, every world, the Tide Key,
  the core, and one for each finale's lesson done cleanly.
