# THE THICKET — galaxy design (draft for approval)

> *The garden has teeth.*

The HARD galaxy. Twelve worlds and a core, built the same way as THE GARDEN
(Medium's galaxy): every world is one ordinary 10:30 run with its own locals,
its own sky, its own finale boss and a relic to win. Everything in it is new —
no enemy, elite or boss from THE GARDEN appears here.

Theme: what a garden becomes when nobody tends it. Briar, thorn, amber,
hives, carnivorous plants, and things that hunt.

---

## How the galaxies fit together

| Galaxy | Difficulty | Opens when |
|---|---|---|
| THE SHALLOWS | Easy | always open *(built later)* |
| THE GARDEN | Medium | always open *(the existing 12 worlds + EVENTIDE)* |
| **THE THICKET** | **Hard** | **you put out THE SOWER at EVENTIDE** |
| THE ROT | Impossible | you put down THE HUNTER at THE HEARTWOOD *(built later)* |

- Existing saves keep everything: every world already taken stays taken in THE GARDEN,
  and anyone who has already beaten EVENTIDE finds THE THICKET open.
- Relics from any galaxy can be equipped anywhere. The two-relic limit stays.
- Each galaxy's gauntlet (the mid-run bosses at 2:45 / 4:45 / 6:45 / 8:45) draws from
  that galaxy's own three **wild bosses**, plus the finales of worlds you've taken *in that galaxy*.

## The rank and file (11)

Each one fills a role one of THE GARDEN's enemies fills, so the spawn system and the
difficulty curve still make sense. What each one actually does is new.

| Enemy | Role | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **THORNLING** | baseline chaser | The Thicket's filler. Quicker and tougher than a Drifter. |
| **BURR** | fast swarm | Rolls in clumps. If it touches you it *latches* and stings for 2s. A dash shakes it off. |
| **BRIARBACK** | tank | Bark plating on its front takes 60% less damage. Flank it. |
| **NETTLE** | ranged kiter | Keeps its distance and fires a fan of three needles. |
| **PODDER** | splitter | Bursts into four Burrs when it dies. |
| **STALKER** | pouncer | Barely visible (a heat-shimmer) until it's within ~300px, then pounces. |
| **HIVE** | spawner | Slow. Releases a Wasp every few seconds while alive, and three more when it dies. |
| **WASP** | darter | Short, fast, straight-line stabs, then a pause. |
| **BURLHEAD** | heavy | Huge and slow. When it stops, it roots for 3s and throws a ring of splinters. |
| **SUNDEW** | zoner | Plants sticky patches that slow you by 35% while you stand in them. |
| **LASHER** | orbiter | Circles at range and snaps a telegraphed vine-line across your path. |

## Elites (4)

| Elite | What it does |
|---|---|
| **GAMEKEEPER** | Aimed volleys. Periodically *marks* you: while you're marked, every Stalker and Burr nearby moves faster. |
| **STAGHORN** | Long charges across the arena, ending in an antler sweep (a crescent of shots). |
| **BROODQUEEN** | Hangs back and summons waves of Wasps, and shields Hives near it. |
| **OLD BRIAR** | Slow, and grows short bramble walls that block movement (not your shots) for a few seconds. |

### Relics

Each has an upside and a cost, like THE GARDEN's. None of them repeats a GARDEN relic.

| Relic | Upside | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **OFFCUT** | Every kill flings a splinter at the nearest enemy. | Max HP −10%. |
| **TAILWIND** | +20% move speed while you haven't been hit for 5s. | Dash cooldown +30%. |
| **BAIT** | Every 12s a lure drops at your feet and draws enemies to it for 3s. | Enemies move 10% faster. |
| **SEGMENT** | Your projectiles pierce one extra enemy. | −15% damage to bosses. |
| **COCOON** | Every level-up heals 25% of max HP. | All other healing −50%. |
| **DIGESTION** | Each hit makes that enemy take +2% more damage from you (up to +20%). | Base damage −10%. |
| **LEECHROOT** | Heal 1 HP for every 40 damage you deal. | You take +15% damage. |
| **BARKSKIN** | Every 8s grow a bark layer that absorbs 15 damage (up to 3). | Pickup radius −30%. |
| **SNARE** | Your dash leaves a snare that roots the first enemy through it for 3s. | Dash cooldown +40%. |
| **AMBERGLASS** | Enemy bullets that come within 60px of you hang frozen for 0.6s first. | Your projectiles travel 20% slower. |
| **HUNTER'S MARK** | The enemy you've hit most is marked and takes +35% damage from you. Killing it moves the mark. | Everything unmarked takes −10%. |
| **SWARMHEART** | Every 5 kills releases a friendly wasp that seeks an enemy for heavy damage. | Weapon area −15%. |

## The finales (12)

Every finale has one signature mechanic that it keeps through three phases, the way
THE GYRE always spins the floor and HOARFROST always builds rime.

**THE PRUNER** · Hedgemoor · ★1
*Two blades on one hinge. It keeps the paths open, whether or not anything is standing on them.*
- **Signature — SNIPS:** two long telegraphed lines cross the arena and close like shears. Get out of the wedge.
- P1: one snip every few seconds, aimed at you, plus slow fans of clippings.
- P2: double snips in an X. Clippings leave short-lived cuttings on the floor.
- P3: the blades unhinge and hunt separately, as two bodies on one health bar.

**THE DANDELION** · Thistledown · ★1
*One breath and it is everywhere.*
- **Signature — GUSTS:** an arrow telegraphs a direction, then a 2s gust pushes you, enemies and every bullet that way.
- P1: gusts, plus drifting clouds of seed-shots that ride the wind.
- P2: seeds that land take root as Burrs.
- P3: stripped to its stalk (faster, frailer), the wind now turns continuously into a slow vortex.

**THE LURE** · Lanternfen · ★2
*It only needs you to want something.*
- **Signature — BAIT:** it seeds false motes among the real ones. They're marked by a warmer glow and a thin gold stalk. Grab one and a jaw snaps shut on you.
- P1: its lantern pulses out rings of shots, with three baits out at a time.
- P2: it sinks, and only the lantern is left moving and firing. The jaws surface wherever the lantern was a moment ago.
- P3: it swallows the lantern. Every bait on the field detonates at once, then it hunts with the jaws.

**THE THOUSANDFOOT** · Wormwood · ★2
*It has been under you the whole time.*
- **Signature — BURROW:** a long segmented body that dives and surfaces through telegraphed holes. Every segment is a turret.
- P1: it surfaces in arcs across the arena, and segments fire sideways as they pass.
- P2: break it in the middle and it splits into two shorter worms.
- P3: it stops surfacing on the arcs. Instead, the head bursts up directly beneath you after a 0.9s ground warning.

**THE CHRYSALIS** · Mothwing Vale · ★2
*Kill it before it finishes becoming.*
- **Signature — HATCH:** it starts as a cocoon on a 60-second clock. However much health it has left when it hatches is what the moth gets, so damage now makes the second half easier.
- P1 (cocoon): hangs still, sweeps silk lines, and hatchlings crawl out.
- P2 (moth): each wingbeat scatters scale-dust, shots that hang and drift for 3s.
- P3: the dust ignites in chains, running along the lines it has drawn.

**THE PITCHER** · Pitcherdeep · ★3
*It does not chase. It does not need to.*
- **Signature — THE LID:** acid rises in from the arena wall. The safe circle shrinks while the lid is closed, then recedes.
- P1: flood to 80% of the arena for 10s, with nectar shots lobbed out of the pitcher.
- P2: flood to 60%, and the nectar leaves slow-pools.
- P3: flood to 45%. It lifts off the ground and drifts after you, and the acid only recedes when you hit the lid on top of it.

**THE STRANGLER** · Stranglehold · ★3
*It holds on. That is all it has ever done.*
- **Signature — THE VINE:** a vine tethers you to it. You move freely inside the leash but can't leave it, and it drains you while it's taut. Dash *across* the vine to cut it; it re-attaches a few seconds later.
- P1: one vine on a long leash, plus fans of thorns.
- P2: it plants two anchor trees and you're held between two vines.
- P3: the leash shortens every few seconds. Cut it, or be crushed.

**THE BURL** · Burlwood · ★3
*Every ring is a year it refused to die.*
- **Signature — RINGS:** layered bark rings act as shields, broken one at a time. Stop hitting it for 3s and the outer ring starts growing back.
- P1: three rings, with a burst of splinters each time one breaks.
- P2: the rings turn, with gaps, and it fires out through the gaps.
- P3: the heartwood is bare. It roots and sends root-lines across the floor in a grid.

**THE TRAPPER** · The Snarelands · ★4
*You will not see the one that gets you.*
- **Signature — SNARES:** it lays hidden snares that show for a moment each time it pulses (every ~6s), or when you get within 60px. Step in one and you're rooted for 1s.
- P1: bola throws (two linked, spinning shots). Six snares at a time, and a dash breaks a snare.
- P2: snares are chained, so springing one springs every snare within 250px.
- P3: it goes into the brush. Only its eyes show, and its own pulses light it up. Dash no longer breaks a snare.

**THE AMBERWRIGHT** · The Amber Sea · ★5 · *holds THE AMBER*
*It keeps everything. It has never let a single thing go.*
- **Signature — SUSPEND:** its shots stop mid-flight and hang as amber beads. On its cue, every bead lets go at once.
- P1: fans that hang, then release on a three-count.
- P2: it catches *your* missed shots too, and throws them back at reduced damage.
- P3: it seals itself in amber. Only its own beads can crack the shell, so dodge the release in a way that sends the beads into it.

**THE WHITE STAG** · The Staglands · ★5 · *holds THE ANTLER* · press on
*They have hunted it for ten thousand years. It has let them.*
- **Signature — THE CHARGE:** telegraphed lanes, then it charges across the arena and leaves thorn furrows behind it.
- P1: single charges, plus an antler sweep (a crescent of shots).
- P2: charges bounce off the wall twice, and the furrows grow into brambles.
- P3: it disappears. Only its glowing hoofprints show where it's walking, and it charges out of nowhere.

**THE COMB** · The Wild Comb · ★5 · *holds THE STING* · press on
*Six sides, forever.*
- **Signature — CELLS:** hex cells tile the floor and fill up over time, and a full cell hatches Wasps. Stand in a cell for 1s to seal it.
- P1: the Comb (a floating hexagon) fires hex-rings, and the cells fill slowly.
- P2: one golden royal cell at a time. If it hatches, a BROODQUEEN comes out.
- P3: the Comb breaks into six fragments that orbit you and fire, and cells fill twice as fast.

## Wild bosses (3) — the Thicket's opening gauntlet pool

**THE GRAFT** — *It has no body of its own. It borrows.*
It lives inside an ordinary enemy, which swells, glows and starts firing. Kill the host and
it jumps to another one nearby, keeping its health bar. In its last phase it takes two hosts at once.

**THE MOLT** — *What you killed was only the skin.*
Every 20% of its health it sheds a hollow shell, which keeps firing its last pattern for 8s
while the real one slips away. Read which one is moving.

**THE PACK** — *Three mouths, one hunger.*
Three hounds on one health bar. They circle and come at you from opposite sides, and when
one gets ahead the others howl and speed up. At each third of its health, one hound falls and the rest enrage.

## The key and the core

**THE BRIAR KEY:** THE AMBER (The Amber Sea), THE ANTLER (The Staglands), THE STING (The Wild Comb).
As in THE GARDEN, nothing extra is stored: holding a piece *is* having taken its world.

**THE HEARTWOOD** — *A star, grown over with bark. Something inside it is still beating.*
Sealed until the key is whole. There is nothing in the arena but you and **THE HUNTER**,
fought in four forms like THE SOWER:

| Form | Line | The fight |
|---|---|---|
| **I · THE HUNTER** | *It learned to hunt from everything you killed.* | A great thorned beast that circles the arena's edge and pounces on telegraphs. |
| **II · THE HEDGE** | *There is no way out it did not plant.* | Bramble walls grow into a shifting maze that blocks your shots too. It strikes from inside the walls. |
| **III · THE ROOT** | *It goes down further than you do.* | It dives. Roots erupt under you, and more lash in from the wall. |
| **IV · THE THORN** | *Every rose you ever reached for.* | Small, fast and precise: dense, exact patterns, with no adds and nowhere to hide. |

## Prizes

- **Mastery skin — HEARTWOOD** (*PUT DOWN THE HUNTER*): bark-brown with gold growth rings and a halo of thorns.
  *"You walked out of the woods. The woods did not."*
- **Pet — FAWN** (*earned at THE HEARTWOOD*): a small neon fawn with no fear left in it.
- **Achievements:** THICKET CONQUEROR (take all twelve worlds) · THE BRIAR KEY (assemble it) ·
  OUT OF THE WOODS (put down THE HUNTER).
